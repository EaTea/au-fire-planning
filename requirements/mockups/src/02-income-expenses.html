Income & expenses

What comes in and what you spend. Enter expenses as after-tax spending. The app works out the pre-tax income or withdrawals needed to cover them TAX-7.

Editing any table: click a cell to edit it in place (in Living expenses, the Medical row is mid-edit). Changes save on Enter or when you click away, and the results recalculate. Hover a row to show its ⋯ menu (Duplicate, Delete), as on the childcare row below. Grows at is a dropdown. Choosing a custom option such as "Inflation + … %" turns it into a field for the rate.
