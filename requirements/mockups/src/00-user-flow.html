User flow

How a user moves through the app. First-time users go through the four input steps in order. Returning users land straight on their results. From the results they can drill into the projection or save and compare scenarios. To change an input, they open the inputs panel without leaving the screen they're on.

Grey box: numbered step in the header nav. Dashed box: action available from the results.
Inputs panel (blue): after the first run, inputs are edited in a panel that drops down over the current screen, so the user keeps their place. The steps stay available for full edits.
All data stays on this device. No backup or sync to other services (§10)
